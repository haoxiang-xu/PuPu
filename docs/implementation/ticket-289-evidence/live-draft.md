# Decision Memo: Weekend Book Exchange Pilot

## Decision
Our volunteer committee has approved a two-week pilot of a weekend book exchange, to be held at our library starting 14 September 2026. The program will be coordinated by Ada Chen, with a total budget cap of $500. Participants may exchange books on Saturdays and Sundays during regular library hours. No personal patron data will be collected as part of this pilot.

## Reasons
This initiative aims to encourage community engagement and promote the sharing of books among patrons. By running the exchange on weekends, we expect higher participation from families and working individuals. Limiting personal data collection ensures privacy and aligns with our values.

## Risks
Potential risks include limited participation, exceeding the budget, or challenges managing book inventory. To mitigate costs, volunteers will track expenses closely. Clear guidelines will help manage book exchanges fairly.

## Success Measures
The pilot will be deemed successful if at least 20 books are exchanged and total expenses remain within the $500 budget. Outcomes will be reviewed at the end of the two weeks to inform future decisions.
