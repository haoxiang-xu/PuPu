**Tailwinds (best-sourced):**
- MCP standardization: Anthropic launched 2024-11-25 (primary); now de-facto cross-lab standard, reportedly donated to an "Agentic AI Foundation" Dec 2025 w/ OpenAI/Block (foundation name not primary-verified). Scale stats (97M downloads/mo, 5800+ servers) are SEO-blog, directional only.
- Local models "good enough": open-weight trails closed frontier by ~3-4 months on composite indices; ~zero gap on knowledge, single digits on reasoning; 5 open families at frontier (DeepSeek/Qwen/Kimi/GLM/Mistral). BUT laptop-runnable gap ~18-24mo — don't conflate. Closed still leads: production coding, human-pref, long-horizon agentic.
- Aggregator thesis VALIDATED WITH CAPITAL but at INFRA layer: OpenRouter $113M Series B @ $1.3B (2026-05-26 primary), ~$50M ARR, ~25T weekly tokens. Counter-signal: some argue aggregation value compresses as models converge.

**Labs' structural limits (strongest strategic finding, well-supported by observable scope):**
- Claude Desktop hardcoded to Anthropic API — cannot swap core model (local model only usable as a tool). ChatGPT app-scoped subscription. Lock-in is a BUSINESS RULE presented as technical.
- Labs converging INWARD: OpenAI retired standalone Atlas browser + Sora app, folded everything into one ChatGPT app (2026-07).
- Why they structurally won't aggregate: (a) routing to a competitor/free-local model cannibalizes their inference revenue; (b) subscription is app-scoped unit of monetization; (c) memory/context is the non-portable retention mechanism.
- White space labs WILL NOT occupy: (1) aggregate competitors' models, (2) run arbitrary local/open-weight models as the primary brain, (3) provider-neutral. This is the durable gap.

**Sobering risks:**
- Client layer is LOW-MOAT, LOW-SWITCHING-COST. No verified breakout/acquisition of a desktop AI CLIENT (value went to infra/OpenRouter). Cherry has no account system (sync via WebDAV); switching = copy local files. LobeChat cloud <1% conversion = users don't feel locked in enough to pay.
- Sherlocking is documented + ongoing: OpenAI's Nov 2023 PDF-upload wipeout is the canonical proof. "80% of wrappers die / 200+ cannibalized" figures are SEO-sourced, directional.
- Durable moats a client CAN hold (vs labs can't copy): privacy/local + provider-neutrality + owned distribution + deep workflow integration. NOT UI polish (labs copy that).
